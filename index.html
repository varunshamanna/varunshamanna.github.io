Journey

How I move genomic projects from raw data to usable evidence

Question

Start with the biological question and the surveillance goal

I begin by understanding the organism, sequencing platform, expected outputs, and the public-health or research question. That framing decides whether the workflow needs to optimise for AMR prediction, outbreak tracking, gene expression, or comparative genomics.

  • Clarify the hypothesis, sample context, and decision points.
  • Match the analysis plan to the sequencing technology and biological system.
  • Make sure the workflow answers the real question, not just the easiest one.
