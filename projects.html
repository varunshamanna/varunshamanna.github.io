Genomic Surveillance

Genomic Surveillance of AMR

Contributed to national-scale antimicrobial resistance surveillance by analysing pathogen whole- genome sequence data, linking it with anonymised metadata, and supporting the infrastructure needed for sustained genomic monitoring.

  • Analysed more than 6000 whole-genome sequences of ESKAPE pathogens
  • Built workflows and scripts for routine WGS analysis
  • Helped translate genomic results into public health and research value

Global Surveillance

Global Pneumococcal Sequencing Project

Worked on pneumococcal genome analysis using established surveillance pipelines and contributed curated datasets to global efforts focused on serotypes, lineages, and antimicrobial resistance.

  • Used the GPS pipeline for serotyping, GPSC typing, and AMR prediction
  • Led sub-study work on surface proteins and non-typeable isolates
  • Supported dataset curation for broader surveillance interpretation

Workflow Engineering

Nanopore Analysis Pipelines

Developed protocols and long-read workflows for projects requiring end-to-end nanopore analysis, from raw reads to assembly, polishing, variant calling, and plasmid reconstruction.

  • Worked across basecalling, demultiplexing, assembly, and polishing
  • Built reproducible workflows for routine sequencing activity
  • Extended analysis to plasmid reconstruction and downstream interpretation

Translational Bioinformatics

Elucidata Bioinformatics Work

Supporting cancer and rare disease projects through human WGS, bulk RNA-seq, single-cell RNA-seq, and automation of repeated bioinformatics workflows using AI-assisted tools.

  • Call and annotate somatic and germline variants from human WGS
  • Analyse bulk and single-cell transcriptomics for translational studies
  • Build AI agents to streamline analysis and reporting workflows
