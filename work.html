Approach

The way I structure a genomics project.

01 Question

Start with the biological objective

I begin by understanding the organism, sequencing platform, metadata, and the real decision the analysis needs to support.

02 Design

Build for reproducibility from the start

I choose tools, references, and quality-control steps with reruns, handoffs, and future scale in mind.

03 Analysis

Connect pipeline outputs to biological meaning

The goal is not just a clean workflow, but an interpretation that makes sense in its clinical, epidemiological, or experimental context.

04 Communication

Present results so teams can act on them

I value outputs that are readable, traceable, and useful to collaborators who may not be deep in the code.
